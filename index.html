How to read this tracker

01 / Known contracts ≠ whales

Beacon Deposit Contract, WETH and bridges are infrastructure, not individual investors.

02 / Exchange deposits ≠ sales

A deposit can be custody, collateral, internal rebalancing or a potential sale.

03 / Attribution confidence

Unknown addresses remain unknown. Labels require corroboration from reputable sources.

04 / Read-only by design

No wallet connection, private keys, signatures or trade execution.
